# Financed Emission Mitigation for Home Mortgages Portfolio

## Financed Emission Methodology

>> Financed Emission (FE) = Attribution Factor * Floor Area * Emission per Floor Area

>> Emission Intensity = Financed Emission / Outstanding Amount

- Financed Emission Methodology is measurement on geenhouse gas (GHG) emission that financial institutions are indirectly responsible for lending, investment, and underwriting activities.

### Factor Unit
- Financed Emission = tonnes CO2e
- Attribution Factor = Proportion of Outstanding amount and Property value at origination
- Floor Area = square metres (m^2)
- Emission per floor area = tonnes CO2e / m^2
- Emission Intensity = tonnes CO2e / 1 Million USD
